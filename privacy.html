Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to AyzMods. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed while you navigate our platform. This Privacy Policy details how data handling occurs when accessing our hosted gaming resources, clients, and custom utility bundles.

2. Information We Collect

AyzMods is a free directory system designed to offer direct download paths. We do not require mandatory registration protocols or premium accounts to use our site. However, certain automated system components track general network telemetry:

  • Log Files: Standard collection of IP addresses, browser distributions, Internet Service Provider (ISP) tags, and general timestamps.
  • Cookies: Temporary memory blocks initialized to save interface preferences (such as light/dark mode choices).

3. Third-Party Services & Links

Our infrastructure references safe, external content storage links (such as Mediafire, Google Drive, or official developer source codes). Once you select a download link and leave our domain, we do not manage or control the data handling procedures of external servers.

We highly recommend reviewing the individual deployment structures and privacy rules of external networks when storing custom configurations on your personal devices.

4. File Safety & Storage Integrity

All optimization tools, modification frameworks, and graphic layout structures displayed on AyzMods undergo standard optimization checks. We do not package stealth software or data trackers inside deployment files. Your system operations and account parameters remain completely safe and secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our platform indexes safe gaming modules suitable for general users. We do not intentionally compile or maintain sensitive identification trackers belonging to younger audiences. If any data is accidentally processed, it is immediately cleared from temporary host server records.
